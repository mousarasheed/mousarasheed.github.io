Research

I work in singularity theory and commutative algebra. My thesis studies semigroups attached to quasi-ordinary hypersurfaces and the Abhyankar–Jung theorem, under the supervision of Abdallah Assi. I am also interested in plane curves with one place at infinity, numerical semigroups, Milnor and Tjurina numbers, and Kähler differentials.

Thesis: Semigroups of quasi-ordinary hypersurfaces and classification of plane curves (defence expected in 2027).
